# Song and Recording are separate entities

A Song (the composition: title, lyrics, lyricist and composer credits, written date) and a Recording (one audio version of it: demo, studio or live, with its own audio file, recorded date and production credits) are modelled separately, and Releases contain Recordings rather than Songs. An artist routinely has a demo, a studio take and a live take of the same song, and a single "song with one audio file" forced either duplicated lyrics and credits or a live album that plays the studio audio.
